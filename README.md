# Structural Stability Science (SSS)

**Scientific Repository | Foundational Architecture | Independent Mathematical Research | Scientific Publications | Source Governance | Institutional Access**

**Scientific Founder & Originating Source Authority: Cara Tonucci**

Structural Stability Science (SSS) is the scientific field establishing structural conditions governing stability under Load across admissible system descriptions.

Load (L), Capacity (C), and Boundary (B) are the invariant structural variables of the foundational scientific architecture.

The originating scientific architecture includes the Structural Identification Method (SIM), Dimensional Human Field Theory (DHFT), and the distinct foundational technical spines of Dimensional Human Physics (DHP) and Dimensional Self Architecture (DSA).

SSS also provides the institutional scientific home for an independent mathematical research program addressing order preservation, admissibility, mathematical analysis, dynamical systems, operator stability, observability, and related investigations.

The independent mathematical program retains its own mathematical assumptions, publications, results, and research objectives. Its inclusion within SSS does not establish derivation from DHFT or require its mathematical results to instantiate Load–Capacity–Boundary mechanics.

This GitHub repository provides secondary preservation, technical reference, and navigation to the originating scientific record.

## 2. Independent Mathematical Research Program

**Mathematical Analysis | Order Preservation | Admissibility | Dynamical Systems | Operator Theory | Observability**

The independent mathematical research program comprises published mathematical investigations and continuing research developed by Cara Tonucci.

Its originating November 2026 publication lineage begins with *Order Preservation Under Real Extension: Admissibility Conditions, Counterexamples, and the Distinction Between Iteration and Input Limits.*

Research areas include:

- **Order Preservation Under Real Extension:** admissibility conditions, counterexamples, iteration, and real-input limits.
- **Mathematical Analysis and Dynamical Systems:** stability, continuation, structural conditions, and related mathematical investigations.
- **Navier–Stokes Research:** investigations concerning the original fixed-initial-value three-dimensional Navier–Stokes problem, including conditional results, mathematical obstructions, and unresolved global-continuation objectives.
- **Operator Theory and Observability:** adjoint gain, operator stability, Fourier observability, rank conditions, and associated mathematical investigations.
- **Observation and Dissipation:** mathematical investigations involving observation, dissipation, and continuation estimates.

Individual mathematical publications retain primary authority over their definitions, assumptions, propositions, theorems, computational evidence, and research status.

The institutional placement of the mathematical program within SSS does not establish mathematical equivalence with DHFT or an asserted derivation of independent mathematical results from the foundational theory.
